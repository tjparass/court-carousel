# Court Carousel

**Fair pickleball court rotation, on everyone's phone.** https://courtcarousel.com

Court Carousel is a free web app that runs the boring part of a pickleball game day. The organizer
keeps a roster per group, ticks who showed up, presses **Start Game** and shares a six-letter code
or QR. Every player opens the link on their own phone, taps their name once, and from then on their
court lights up each round. Between rounds the organizer presses **Next Round**; late arrivals,
guests, people leaving, swaps and court-count changes are handled on the spot.

- Balances games played and rests; mixes partners and opponents; rotates everyone across courts
- Shared round timer that chimes when time is up
- 4 to 150 players, 1 to 30 courts; also suits padel, badminton and tennis doubles
- No install, no scores, no ads. Organizers sign in with Google, Microsoft or email; players never need an account
